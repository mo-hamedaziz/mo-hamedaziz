## 🧭 About Me

I'm a **Platform Engineer** from Tunisia 🇹🇳 who loves building cloud-native systems that simply work: reliable, secure, and easy to run.

My corner of the world is where **Platform Engineering, DevOps, and SRE** meet. I spend most of my time with Kubernetes, cloud infrastructure, and automation, and I get a real kick out of taking messy, manual processes and turning them into clean workflows that teams can actually depend on.
